- **Punnapra-Vayalar Rising** — the real 1946 communist revolt in the Alleppey coir belt. Red coastal revolution vs CP's police state.
- **The Diwan's Terror vs Responsible Government** — CP's authoritarian modernisation vs State Congress democratic agitation. Democratic-reform branch, constitutional monarchy.
- **Chemical Powerhouse** — FACT (first Indian fertiliser plant), Travancore Titanium, Indian Aluminium (Aluva), Pallivasal hydro. Heavy-industry-first focus tree.

### Economic & cultural flavor hooks
Monazite/thorium sands (Manavalakurichi); FACT & Travancore Titanium; Pallivasal hydroelectric; Cochin natural harbour (Willingdon Island); Alleppey coir (labour militancy); highest literacy in India; Temple Entry Proclamation 1936; matrilineal Nair society; Nair Brigade / Travancore State Forces (small navy & air squadron); University of Travancore 1937; State Bank of Travancore; Sri Padmanabhaswamy temple treasury (immense hidden wealth); Swathi Thirunal Carnatic music; spice & rubber plantation economy.

- **Sir Ghulam Hussain Hidayatullah** (1879–1948) — first Premier of separated Sindh (1937). HOI4: country_leader. Ideology: authoritarian/democratic (Muslim League).
- **Allah Bakhsh Soomro** (1900–1943) — secular-nationalist Premier, anti-Two-Nation, founded Azad Muslim Conference; assassinated 1943. HOI4: country_leader. Ideology: democratic/secular.

- **Pir Pagaro VI (Sibghatullah Shah Rashdi II)** (1909–1943) — spiritual head of the Hurs; led the Hur rebellion, hanged 1943. HOI4: general / resistance-army leader. Ideology: authoritarian/theocratic.

- **Jamshed Nusserwanjee Mehta** (1886–1952) — "Maker of Modern Karachi," first elected Mayor; Parsi civic reformer. HOI4: political_advisor (civic). Ideology: democratic.
- **Mir Ali Murad Khan Talpur II** — ruling Mir of Khairpur princely state. HOI4: country_leader (Khairpur sub-tag). Ideology: authoritarian monarchy.
- **Mir Naseer Khan Talpur** (d.1845) & **Mir Fateh Ali Khan Talpur** (1758–1802) — Talpur Mirs who ruled Sindh until 1843. HOI4: LEGACY founder tokens.
- **Bhagat Kanwar Ram** (1885–1939) — revered Sindhi Hindu Sufi singer-saint; murdered 1939 (communal flashpoint). HOI4: cultural advisor / event token.
- **Sadhu T. L. Vaswani** (1879–1966) — Sindhi Hindu educationist. HOI4: political_advisor (minority bloc).
- **Sir Arnold Musto** (1883–1975) — chief engineer of the Sukkur (Lloyd) Barrage (1932). HOI4: theorist / high_command (engineering).

### What-if scenarios (grounded)

- **The Hur Rebellion** — the real Hur guerrilla war under Pir Pagaro (1930s–43, martial law, armoured trains). Theocratic Pir-led revolt vs landed Waderos.
- **Karachi, Free Port of the Indus** — Jamshed Mehta's cosmopolitan Karachi as an Arabian-Sea trade republic. Democratic merchant-republic; naval & commerce focuses.
- **The Talpur Restoration** — revive the Mir dynasty deposed at Miani/Hyderabad 1843 (Khairpur as surviving seed). Monarchist reunification branch.
- **Allah Bakhsh's Secular Sindh vs the Two-Nation Theory** — Soomro's composite nationalism vs G. M. Syed / League communalism. Ideological civil-war/election branch (1943 assassination trigger).

### Economic & cultural flavor hooks
Karachi deep-water harbour (grain/cotton export gateway); Sukkur/Lloyd Barrage 1932 (largest irrigation scheme on earth); Sindhi Hindu banking diaspora (Shikarpuri & Hyderabadi Bhaiband financiers); Shah Abdul Latif Bhitai Sufi poetry (Risalo); Hur Jamaat militant order; Manzilgah mosque dispute 1939 (communal flashpoint); Mohenjo-daro/Indus heritage; Waderos (feudal landlords vs haris/tenants); Thar frontier. **NOTE:** avoid inventing oil/gas — Sui gas found only 1952; keep any petroleum angle explicitly speculative.

- **Gopabandhu Das** (1877–1928) — "Utkalmani," social reformer, founded Satyabadi school. LEGACY token.

- **Sriram Chandra Bhanj Deo** (1871–1912) — great modernising Maharaja of Mayurbhanj. LEGACY founder token.
- **Maharaja Rajendra Narayan Singh Deo** (1912–1975) — ruler of Patna (Bolangir), conservative feudal bloc leader. HOI4: country_leader / political_advisor. Ideology: authoritarian.
- **Raja Shankar Pratap Singh Deo** — ruler of Dhenkanal, target of the 1938 Praja Mandal revolt. HOI4: country_leader (feudatory) / antagonist.
- **Sarangadhar Das** (1887–1957) — US-educated engineer, Praja Mandal & socialist organiser. HOI4: political_advisor. Ideology: socialist/communist.
- **Nilakantha Das** (1884–1967) — poet-politician, Odia language nationalism. HOI4: political_advisor.

### What-if scenarios (grounded)
- **Utkal Sammilani — One Odia Nation** — the real 1903–36 movement uniting scattered Odia tracts & 20+ princely states. Nation-formation focus tree from a fractured Garjat patchwork.
- **The Garjat Rising (Praja Mandal 1938–39)** — real peasant revolts against feudatory Rajas (Dhenkanal, Nilgiri, Ranpur; Baji Rout martyrdom). Princes vs Praja Mandal → democratic/socialist republic or feudal crackdown.

- **Mayurbhanj's Model State** — Sriram Chandra's modernisation as template: a princely state that industrialises and out-competes the plains Congress. Enlightened-monarchy branch.

### Economic & cultural flavor hooks
Sukinda chromite belt; Mayurbhanj/Keonjhar iron ore feeding Tata steel; Talcher coal; bauxite; Mahanadi delta rice & dam potential; Jagannath Temple of Puri (Gajapati as "first servant" — sacral legitimacy); Konark Sun Temple; first language-based state (Odia nationalism, 1936); Cuttack silver filigree & Pattachitra; Utkal Tannery/leather; Chilika lagoon; Garjat feudatory patchwork (Rajas vs Praja Mandal).

- **The Grouping Crisis** — Bordoloi's real 1946 stand refusing grouping with Bengal. Resist absorption by a larger Bengali/eastern power.

### Economic & cultural flavor hooks
Digboi oil & AOC refinery (Asia's first); tea gardens (Assam CTC, planter economy, indentured labour); tungsten/coal/limestone in the hills; Brahmaputra riverine logistics & floods; "line system" & migration tension (Bengali Muslim settlers vs indigenous); Bihu festival; Ahom heritage (Rang Ghar, Kareng Ghar, Talatal Ghar); Kaziranga; the Burma gateway (later Ledo/Stilwell Road); Sattriya/Vaishnavite Sankardev monastic culture.
