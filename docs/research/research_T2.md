## HIN — Awadh / Ganga Federation (Lucknow, Gangetic plain; Oudh / United Provinces)

### Historical figures (validated)
- **Wajid Ali Shah** (1822–1887) — last Nawab of Awadh (r. 1847–1856), deposed/exiled to Metiabruz; patron of Kathak, thumri, Urdu theatre. HOI4: country_leader (legacy token). Ideology: authoritarian/despotism.
- **Begum Hazrat Mahal** (c.1820–1879) — wife of Wajid Ali Shah; led 1857 uprising in Awadh, ruled Lucknow as regent, died in exile in Kathmandu. HOI4: country_leader / general. Ideology: authoritarian nationalist.
- **Birjis Qadr** (1845–1893) — son of Wajid Ali Shah & Hazrat Mahal; proclaimed Wali of Awadh 1857 under his mother's regency. HOI4: country_leader (restored heir). Ideology: authoritarian.
- **Asaf-ud-Daula** (r. 1775–1797) — Nawab who moved capital to Lucknow, built Bara Imambara. HOI4: legacy token.
- **Maulvi Ahmadullah Shah** (c.1787–1858) — "Maulvi of Faizabad," spiritual-military commander of 1857 revolt. HOI4: general / theorist.
- **Rana Beni Madho Singh** (d. c.1859–60) — powerful Awadh taluqdar, unyielding 1857 commander. HOI4: field_marshal / general.
- **Raja Jai Lal Singh** (d. 1859) — Awadh courtier and 1857 rebel leader in Lucknow. HOI4: general / high_command.
- **Babu Kunwar Singh** (1777–1858) — zamindar of Jagdishpur, aged 1857 commander. HOI4: field_marshal.
- **Sir Syed Ahmad Khan** (1817–1898) — modernist reformer, founder of Aligarh MAO College (AMU). HOI4: political_advisor / theorist.
- **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (1861–1946) — founder of Banaras Hindu University, Hindu Mahasabha president. HOI4: political_advisor / country_leader. Ideology: democratic / Hindu-nationalist.
- **Motilal Nehru** (1861–1931) — Allahabad barrister, Congress leader. HOI4: political_advisor (liberal).
- **Jawaharlal Nehru** (1889–1964) — Allahabad; leading Gangetic nationalist. HOI4: country_leader. Ideology: democratic/socialist.
- **Acharya Narendra Deva** (1889–1956) — Congress Socialist Party theorist. HOI4: theorist / country_leader. Ideology: communism/socialism.
- **Chandra Shekhar Azad** (1906–1931) — HSRA revolutionary. HOI4: high_command / intel.
- **Ram Prasad Bismil** (1897–1927) & **Ashfaqulla Khan** (1900–1927) — Kakori action leaders. HOI4: intel / high_command.
- **Raja Mahendra Pratap** (1886–1979) — founded Provisional Government of India in Kabul (1915). HOI4: country_leader (exile-provisional). Ideology: socialist/nationalist. Alive 1936+.
- **Tej Bahadur Sapru** (1875–1949) — Allahabad liberal constitutionalist. HOI4: political_advisor (liberal). Alive 1936+.
- **Govind Ballabh Pant** (1887–1961) — UP Congress leader/administrator. HOI4: political_advisor / country_leader (democratic). Alive 1936+.

### What-if scenarios (grounded)
- **The Restored Wali of Awadh** — Without British annexation, the Nishapuri dynasty rules a sovereign Awadh from Lucknow; Birjis Qadr's line succeeds, rebuilds the disbanded army. Unlocks: dynastic legitimacy tree + army rebuild.
- **The Taluqdar Confederation** — Awadh's landed magnates form a feudal military federation vs a centralizing reform faction. Unlocks: decentralized-levy vs centralized-conscription civil-war fork.
- **Ganga-Jamuni Federation** — Building on Awadh's syncretic Shia-Hindu court culture, Lucknow leads a Gangetic-plain federation. Unlocks: federation diplomacy + Hindi-Urdu composite spirit.
- **The 1857 Legacy Rising** — Revanchist mobilization of the Great Rebellion's memory into a war of "completion." Unlocks: nationalist war goal + guerrilla doctrine.
- **The Revolutionary Underground** — Kakori/HSRA veterans drive a radical-left seizure. Unlocks: communist coup path + intel/partisan focus.
- **Aligarh vs Banaras** — Sir Syed's modernist Aligarh vs Malaviya's Hindu-revivalist Banaras culture-war axis. Unlocks: mutually exclusive education/ideology spirits.

### Economic & cultural flavor hooks
- Lucknow nawabi culture: Kathak (Lucknow gharana), thumri, Urdu poetry, chikankari, dum pukht cuisine.
- Shia infrastructure: Bara & Chhota Imambara, Muharram — legitimacy/stability spirits.
- Agrarian base: taluqdari zamindari (giant Gangetic estates).
- Institutions: Aligarh Muslim University, Banaras Hindu University, Allahabad University.
- Tensions: Hindu-Muslim UP balance, taluqdar-vs-peasant land pressure, Shia-Sunni Lucknow dynamics.

## HYD — Hyderabad (Asaf Jahi Nizam's dominion; Deccan / Telangana-Marathwada-Karnataka)

### Historical figures (validated)
- **Mir Osman Ali Khan, Asaf Jah VII** (1886–1967) — 7th and last Nizam (r. 1911–1948); reputedly the world's richest man; founder of Osmania University, Nizamsagar dam; issued own currency; Muslim ruler of Hindu-majority state. HOI4: country_leader. Ideology: authoritarian/despotism. Alive 1936+.
- **Mir Mahbub Ali Khan, Asaf Jah VI** (1866–1911) — previous Nizam, Osman's father. HOI4: legacy token.
- **Salar Jung I (Mir Turab Ali Khan)** (1829–1883) — celebrated reforming PM (Diwan): administration, revenue, railways, police. HOI4: legacy token / advisor archetype.
- **Salar Jung III (Mir Yousuf Ali Khan)** (1889–1949) — PM 1912–14, art collector. HOI4: political_advisor. Alive 1936+.
- **Mir Laik Ali** (1904–1971) — last PM of Hyderabad (1947–48); engineer-industrialist; led government during Operation Polo. HOI4: country_leader / head-of-government (technocratic-authoritarian). Alive 1936+.
- **Syed Kasim Razvi** (1902–1970) — Majlis-e-Ittehad-ul-Muslimeen leader and Razakar commander; hardline resistance. HOI4: country_leader / political_advisor (militia strongman). Ideology: fascism. Alive 1936+.
- **Bahadur Yar Jung** (1905–1944) — founder-orator of Ittehad-ul-Muslimeen. HOI4: theorist / political_advisor (Islamist-authoritarian).
- **Major General Syed Ahmed El Edroos** (1899–1962) — C-in-C of Hyderabad State Forces; Hadhrami-Arab descent. HOI4: field_marshal / army_chief. Alive 1936+.
- **Maharaja Kishen Pershad ("Shad")** (1864–1940) — twice Hindu PM (Diwan), Urdu/Persian poet; composite culture. HOI4: political_advisor (unity).
- **Sir Akbar Hydari** (1869–1942) — finance member and PM; modernizing administrator. HOI4: political_advisor (economist).
- **Ahmad Said Khan, Nawab of Chhatari** (1888–1982) — acting PM in the 1940s. HOI4: political_advisor / high_command. Alive 1936+.
- **Swami Ramananda Tirtha** (1903–1972) — leader of Hyderabad State Congress. HOI4: country_leader (opposition) / advisor. Ideology: democratic. Alive 1936+.
- **Puchalapalli Sundarayya** (1913–1985) — communist leader of the Telangana armed rebellion. HOI4: country_leader / high_command. Ideology: communism. Alive 1936+.
- **Ravi Narayana Reddy** (1908–1991) — communist / Andhra Mahasabha leader. HOI4: high_command / advisor. Alive 1936+.
- **Makhdoom Mohiuddin** (1908–1969) — Urdu poet and communist organizer of Telangana. HOI4: theorist / advisor. Alive 1936+.

### What-if scenarios (grounded)
- **The Sovereign Nizamate (Osmanistan)** — Nizam's real 1947–48 independence bid; keeps currency and railway, seeks a sea outlet. Unlocks: independence war-goal + neutral-power diplomacy.
- **The Razakar State** — Kasim Razvi's militia seizes power, sidelining the aristocratic cabinet. Unlocks: fascist coup path + paramilitary spirit (Razakar excesses cost war support).
- **The Telangana People's War** — the real 1946–51 communist peasant rebellion succeeds, overthrowing the Nizam and deshmukh class. Unlocks: communist civil-war fork + land redistribution.
- **The Ganga-Jamuni Reform** — Kishen Pershad's Hindu-premier legacy + Salar Jung reform defuse the ~85% Hindu majority under Muslim rule. Unlocks: reform vs reaction fork.
- **The Salar Jung Restoration** — technocratic modernization (Singareni coal, Osmania science, railways). Unlocks: industrial focus tree + civil-service reform.
- **March of the Deccan** — Hyderabad leverages wealth + El Edroos's army to contest the Maratha hegemon for the southern plateau. Unlocks: regional-war branch vs the Maratha core.

### Economic & cultural flavor hooks
- Own currency (Hyderabadi/Osmania Sicca rupee) — economic-sovereignty spirit.
- Nizam's Guaranteed State Railway — logistics bonus.
- Resources: Singareni Collieries (coal); historic Golconda/Kollur diamond mines — mining/treasury hooks.
- Institutions: Osmania University (Urdu-medium), Osmania General Hospital, Nizamsagar dam.
- Tensions: Muslim ruling minority over Hindu majority; Arya Samaj agitation (1938–39); State Congress vs Ittehad; Telangana communist uprising; deshmukh/jagirdar landlordism; Arab (Hadhrami) and Pathan mercenaries.

## PJB — Sikh Empire (Punjab, capital Lahore; Khalsa Raj)

### Historical figures (validated)
- **Maharaja Ranjit Singh** (1780–1839) — "Sher-e-Punjab," founder of the Sikh Empire; secular-tolerant absolutist. HOI4: country_leader (founding legacy token). Ideology: authoritarian/despotism.
- **Hari Singh Nalwa** (1791–1837) — greatest Khalsa general and C-in-C; conquered Peshawar/Khyber; killed at Jamrud. HOI4: field_marshal (legacy) / army_chief namesake.
- **Sham Singh Attariwala** (1790–1846) — veteran Sikh general, died at Sabraon. HOI4: general / field_marshal (legacy).
- **Akali Phula Singh** (1761–1823) — Nihang-Akali warrior-leader, irregular commander. HOI4: general / theorist (irregular/spiritual).
- **Zorawar Singh Kahluria** (1786–1841) — Dogra general ("Napoleon of India"), conquered Ladakh/Baltistan, campaigned Tibet. HOI4: field_marshal (mountain-warfare legacy).
- **Maharani Jind Kaur (Rani Jindan)** (1817–1863) — regent-mother of Duleep Singh, the "rebel queen." HOI4: country_leader / advisor (regent). Ideology: authoritarian nationalist.
- **Maharaja Duleep Singh** (1838–1893) — last Maharaja of the Sikh Empire; exiled to Britain. HOI4: country_leader (exiled/restored heir).
- **Ventura, Allard, Avitabile, Court** — European mercenary generals who built the Fauj-i-Khas. HOI4: foreign general / theorist (military modernization).
- **Master Tara Singh** (1885–1967) — foremost Shiromani Akali Dal / Panthic leader. HOI4: country_leader / advisor. Ideology: Sikh nationalism. Alive 1936+.
- **Baba Kharak Singh** (1868–1963) — early Akali leader, first SGPC president. HOI4: political_advisor (Panthic). Alive 1936+.
- **Giani Kartar Singh** (1902–1974) — Akali strategist and negotiator. HOI4: advisor / high_command. Alive 1936+.
- **Sir Fazl-i-Husain** (1877–1936) — founder of the cross-communal Unionist Party; died 1936. HOI4: advisor / country_leader. Ideology: democratic. (short expire)
- **Sir Sikandar Hayat Khan** (1892–1942) — Unionist Premier of Punjab (1937–42). HOI4: country_leader. Ideology: democratic/authoritarian agrarian. Alive 1936, expire 1942.
- **Sir Khizar Hayat Khan Tiwana** (1900–1975) — last Unionist Premier; resisted partition. HOI4: country_leader. Ideology: democratic. Alive 1936+.
- **Sir Chhotu Ram** (1881–1945) — Unionist Jat peasant leader, agrarian reformer. HOI4: advisor (agrarian). Alive 1936+.
- **Sardar Baldev Singh** (1902–1961) — Panthic Party leader. HOI4: advisor / high_command. Alive 1936+.
- **Lala Har Dayal** (1884–1939) — founder-ideologue of the Ghadar Party. HOI4: theorist (revolutionary). Alive 1936+.
- **Sohan Singh Bhakna** (1870–1968) — first president of the Ghadar Party. HOI4: high_command / advisor. Alive 1936+.
- **Udham Singh** (1899–1940) — assassinated Michael O'Dwyer (1940). HOI4: intel / high_command.
- **Bhagat Singh** (1907–1931) — HSRA revolutionary, Lahore; executed 1931. HOI4: intel / theorist (revolutionary-socialist icon).
- **Lala Lajpat Rai** (1865–1928) — "Punjab Kesari," Arya Samaj/nationalist leader. HOI4: advisor / theorist.

### What-if scenarios (grounded)
- **The Second Khalsa Raj** — with no 1849 annexation, the Sikh Empire survives under a restored Ranjit Singh dynasty (Duleep Singh / Jind Kaur line); the Lahore Durbar rebuilds the Khalsa Army. Unlocks: dynastic-restoration tree + European-drilled modernization.
- **The Panthic State** — grounded in the Akali/SGPC movement, Master Tara Singh's faction pushes a Sikh-sovereign polity. Unlocks: Sikh-nationalist path + gurdwara-reform mobilization.
- **The Unionist Compromise** — the real Unionist Party (Fazl-i-Husain → Sikandar → Khizar) holds Punjab's Muslim-Sikh-Hindu balance against communal partition. Unlocks: unity-government path + agrarian reform.
- **The Ghadar Rising** — the real 1914–15 Ghadar mutiny plan succeeds. Unlocks: revolutionary-republic path + diaspora-support mechanic.
- **March of the Khalsa** — reviving Nalwa's and Zorawar Singh's conquests, Punjab drives to retake Peshawar, the Khyber, Ladakh/Kashmir. Unlocks: frontier war-goals + mountain doctrine.
- **The Partition Crisis** — Punjab's communal fault lines fracture the province among Sikh, Muslim (League), and Hindu blocs. Unlocks: three-way civil-war fork + population-transfer events.

### Economic & cultural flavor hooks
- Canal colonies (Chenab/Lyallpur, Sandal Bar, 1880s) — the subcontinent's breadbasket; agriculture/settlement project.
- Martial-races recruitment: premier military-manpower reservoir (Sikhs, Punjabi Muslims, Jats, Dogras) — manpower spirit.
- Lahore: capital, Lahore Fort, arsenal, Government College — administrative/industrial hub.
- Religious-institutional: Golden Temple/Harmandir Sahib + SGPC gurdwara network (1920s Gurdwara Reform / Akali movement) — legitimacy/mobilization mechanic.
- Tensions: Sikh-Muslim-Hindu tripartite balance; Akali vs Unionist vs Muslim League; Arya Samaj Hindu revivalism; martial-races vs peasant-tenancy class question.
